# Yinghao - High-Leverage Life, Career and Automation System

## North Star

Build a sustainable long-term personal operating system that compounds three forms of capital:

1. **Health capital** — strength, cardiovascular fitness, sleep, energy and longevity.
2. **Knowledge capital** — English, AI/data skills, reading and deep understanding.
3. **Career capital** — a durable combination of supply chain, trade/compliance, data, AI and automation.

The goal is not to fill every day with more tasks. The goal is to progressively reduce low-value manual work, build reusable systems, and reinvest saved time into health, learning, career and life.

---

## 1. Long-Term Career Direction

Build on existing experience rather than restart from zero:

**Supply Chain + Procurement + Logistics + Customs/Trade Compliance + Data + AI + Automation**

Suggested progression:

Supply Chain / Procurement / Logistics
→ Trade & Customs Compliance
→ Excel / Power Query / Power BI / SQL / Python
→ AI research, analysis and document processing
→ Automation / APIs / AI agents
→ **AI-enabled Supply Chain & Trade/Compliance Specialist**

The competitive advantage comes from the combination of domain experience and technology, not from becoming a generic AI specialist.

---

## 2. Use Real Work as the AI Laboratory

Do not separate work, learning and AI practice.

Whenever a recurring task appears — RFQs, supplier comparisons, freight quotations, invoices, HS classification, customs documents, inventory, POs, price histories or follow-ups — ask:

> If this happens again, can a machine do 50% of it?

Then:

> Can the system eventually do 80%, leaving only judgment and approval to me?

Target workflow:

Incoming email/file
→ AI extracts information
→ structured data
→ automated comparison/check
→ exception/risk flag
→ AI recommendation/draft
→ human review and approval

Human effort should increasingly move from **execution → judgment → system design**.

---

## 3. Automation Ladder

Use the simplest sufficient solution:

1. **Delete** — does this task need to exist?
2. **Simplify** — checklist, template, standard folder/process.
3. **AI-assist** — research, extraction, comparison, checking, drafting.
4. **No-code automation** — Excel, Power Query, Power Automate.
5. **Python/API** — only for stable, valuable, recurring workflows.
6. **Agent workflow** — AI reads, analyses and prepares actions; human approves high-impact decisions.

Avoid spending ten hours automating a ten-minute low-frequency task.

## 4. Learning System

### English — 20–30 min/day

Use:

**Input → Active Recall → Speak → Correction → Repeat**

Prefer practical speaking and explanation over passive vocabulary collection.

### AI/Data — 30–45 min/day

Use roughly:

**20% structured learning + 80% projects**

Every major skill should create a useful output, e.g.:

- Supplier quotation analyser
- Freight comparison tool
- Customs-document checker
- Procurement dashboard
- HS classification research workflow

### Reading — 20–30 min/day

Focus on durable domains such as history, anthropology, biology, AI, economics, psychology, decision-making and business.

For important books retain:

- 3 important ideas
- 1 belief/change in understanding
- 1 action

## 5. Health System

Optimise for consistency and longevity rather than a perfect programme.

Core pillars:

- Strength
- Cardiovascular fitness
- Sleep/recovery
- Sustainable nutrition

Simple weekly baseline:

- Mon — Strength 30–40 min
- Tue — Run/brisk walk 30 min
- Wed — Strength 30–40 min
- Thu — Light movement/recovery
- Fri — Swim/cardio
- Sat — Long walk, hiking or cycling
- Sun — Recovery

Design the system so it remains realistic decades from now.

---

## 6. Daily Rule of Three

A successful day needs only three meaningful wins:

### Career
One task that protects or increases career capital.

### Learning
One English / AI / data / reading action.

### Health
One exercise, recovery, sleep or nutrition action.

Everything else is secondary.

---

## 7. Weekly CEO Review — 20 Minutes

Ask:

1. What was the most important result this week?
2. What wasted the most time?
3. What recurring task can be simplified, automated or delegated?
4. What did I actually learn?
5. What are next week's three most important outcomes?

Score only the broad trend:

- Health /10
- Learning /10
- Career /10
- Life /10

Do not over-engineer tracking.

---

## 8. One New Lever Every Month

Create at least one reusable asset/system every month.

Examples:

1. RFQ comparison template
2. Freight quotation analyser
3. Customs-document checker
4. Supplier follow-up workflow
5. HS classification research workflow
6. Procurement dashboard
7. AI supplier research workflow

The objective is not merely to study for twelve months. It is to finish the year with twelve assets that continue producing value.

---

## 9. Delegation Rule

For each task ask:

**Is it important?**

- No → delete/reduce it.
- Yes → ask whether Yinghao personally needs to do it.

If not → delegate, automate or AI-assist.

Reserve personal attention for:

- Judgment
- Negotiation
- Risk
- Relationships
- Decisions
- Final approval

---

## 10. 90-Day Execution Cycle

Keep the long-term direction broad but execute in 90-day cycles.

Each cycle has only three major outcomes:

### Health
Establish/maintain a reliable four-session weekly movement and exercise rhythm.

### Learning
Build practical English and AI/data capability through active practice and projects.

### Career
Complete one portfolio-quality supply-chain/trade/data/AI automation project.

At the end of 90 days: review results, retain what worked, remove unnecessary complexity, and define the next three outcomes.

---

## Core Principle

> Do not optimise life by filling every available hour. Build systems that progressively reduce what must be done manually, while increasing health, knowledge and career capital.

The long-term compounding loop is:

**Learn → Apply to real work → Systemise → Automate/delegate → Save time → Reinvest in health/learning/career → Repeat.**
