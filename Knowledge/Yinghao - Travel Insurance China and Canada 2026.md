# Yinghao - Travel Insurance China and Canada 2026

## Scope
Travel insurance review for Yinghao's 2026 China and Canada trips, including cycling and rental-car risk.

## China trip — Cover-More International Basic

### Policy snapshot
- Destination: China
- Travel dates: 24 Nov 2026 to 15 Dec 2026
- Travellers: Yinghao Wang and Jinhao Zhu
- Plan: Cover-More International Basic
- Premium: AUD 259.38
- Excess: AUD 250
- Cancellation cover selected: AUD 0

### What is covered well
- Overseas medical expenses: Unlimited
- Emergency dental: up to AUD 2,000, but only for emergency treatment to relieve pain or temporarily restore function
- Cycling / cycle touring (leisure): automatically included, so injuries from ordinary leisure cycling can fall under overseas medical cover
- Basic baggage / personal-effects cover exists, but limits are much lower than comprehensive plans

### Important limitations
- No Rental Vehicle Insurance Excess cover under Basic
- Routine medical or dental treatment is not covered
- Planned / routine dental work such as normal fillings, cleaning, check-ups or cosmetic dentistry is not covered
- Routine ear cleaning / earwax removal is generally not covered unless an unexpected illness or symptomatic condition requires medically necessary treatment
- Cancellation cover is AUD 0
- Bicycle theft is relatively weak: items left unattended in public places are generally excluded; Cover-More does not provide the same explicit secure-bike-lock exception as Southern Cross

### Practical interpretation
Use this policy mainly as low-cost emergency medical protection, not as comprehensive trip protection. It is adequate for unexpected illness, injury and leisure-cycling injury, but weaker for rental cars, cancellation, high-value baggage and bicycle theft.

## Canada trip — Southern Cross Travel Insurance International Comprehensive

### Core benefits
- Overseas medical and evacuation: Unlimited
- Emergency dental: AUD 2,000 per person
- Cancellation / changing journey before departure: standard AUD 2,500 per person, can be increased
- Changes to journey after departure: up to AUD 50,000 per person
- Travel interruption: up to AUD 30,000 per person
- Baggage and personal items: AUD 25,000 per person
- Unspecified phone / laptop / tablet / camera: AUD 3,000 per item, up to AUD 10,000 total per person
- Personal accident: AUD 50,000 per person (age 16–80)
- Personal liability: AUD 1,000,000 per person
- Rental vehicle excess: AUD 10,000 per person
- SCTI does not deduct its own policy excess from rental vehicle excess claims

### Rental-car structure
SCTI rental-vehicle cover is not a replacement for the rental company's vehicle insurance. It only reimburses the rental vehicle excess / deductible that the renter becomes liable to pay.

Conditions include:
- Vehicle rented from a licensed rental company
- Driver must be named on the rental agreement
- Driver must also be insured under the SCTI policy
- Must follow the rental agreement and local driving laws
- Must have taken out vehicle insurance with the rental company

Not covered:
- Off-road driving; cover is for formed / paved roads or car parks
- Motor-vehicle third-party liability under SCTI personal liability
- Incorrectly charged rental excess
- Cases where the renter declined vehicle insurance

### How it complements the Zuzuche rental package
The intended structure is:
1. Rental company's CDW / theft / third-party liability
2. Zuzuche supplementary cover
3. SCTI rental vehicle excess as an additional backup layer

Remaining possible gaps include administration fees, loss-of-use fees and some special roadside / damage charges not treated as rental excess.

## Flight delay / interruption — Canada
SCTI is stronger than a simple fixed-delay benefit. If an unexpected event interrupts the journey, it may reimburse the higher of:
- original unused prepaid travel costs, less refunds / credits; or
- reasonable additional costs required to rearrange the journey, less refunds / credits.

Examples include additional accommodation, meals / transport and forfeited prepaid accommodation or tours. Travel interruption is up to AUD 30,000 per person.

SCTI expects travellers to first seek refunds, credits or compensation from airlines, hotels and other providers, then claims the remaining eligible loss.

## China vs Canada insurance conclusion

### China — Cover-More Basic
Best viewed as:
- emergency medical focused
- low premium
- suitable for leisure cycling injury
- weak for rental vehicle excess, cancellation and bicycle theft

### Canada — SCTI Comprehensive
Clearly stronger overall because it adds:
- AUD 10,000 rental vehicle excess
- AUD 25,000 baggage cover per person
- stronger interruption / journey-change protection
- much more useful protection for a driving-heavy trip

## Key rules to remember
- Keep all receipts and written reports
- For theft / loss, report to police, hotel, airline or relevant authority promptly and obtain written evidence
- For major medical treatment, contact the insurer's emergency assistance team as early as possible
- Do not assume routine medical or dental maintenance is claimable
- For rental cars, every actual driver must be listed on the rental agreement and comply with its terms
- For airline disruption, claim refunds / credits from the airline first, then claim eligible unrecovered losses from the insurer
