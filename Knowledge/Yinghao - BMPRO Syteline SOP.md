# Yinghao - BMPRO Syteline SOP

## Book in BMPRO US stock

> [!warning]
> Before receiving BMPRO US stock, change the active warehouse to the relevant BMPRO US warehouse. After the receipt is completed, always change it back to **Main (BMPRO Australia)**.

### Procedure

1. In Infor ERP SL (Syteline), open **Actions → Change Warehouse**.
2. Select the relevant **BMPRO US warehouse**.
3. Open **Purchase Order Receiving**.
4. Enter the PO number and complete the stock receipt/book-in.
5. Check that the receipt has been posted to the correct US warehouse.
6. Open **Actions → Change Warehouse** again.
7. Change the warehouse back to **Main (BMPRO Australia)**.

### Quick reminder

**US warehouse → Purchase Order Receiving → Book in → Main**

### Common error to avoid

Do not book in US stock while the active warehouse is still **Main**, and do not leave Syteline set to the US warehouse after the transaction.
