# Yinghao - 90-Day English and AI Learning System

## Goal
Build a practical 90-day system to improve English and AI together by using English to learn AI, and AI to train English.

Core principle:

> Less passive input, more active recall. Less collecting, more output.

## 90-Day Outcomes

### English
- Understand most clear, slower AI and work-related English content.
- Speak continuously for 5–10 minutes about work, AI and daily topics.
- Actively master roughly 600–900 useful high-frequency, work and AI words/expressions.
- Draft work emails and short analyses independently before using AI for correction.

### AI
- Understand core concepts such as LLM, Transformer, token, embedding, RAG, agent and API.
- Independently design prompts, research questions and simple AI workflows.
- Understand basic Python/API/JSON concepts.
- Complete several small practical AI projects.

## Weekly Structure — 6 + 1

### Monday — Learn
- English: 20–25 min listening/reading; keep only 5–8 useful expressions.
- AI: 20–25 min on one core concept.
- Close the material and recall the key ideas from memory.

### Tuesday — Recall
Without notes:
- Recall yesterday’s English expressions and create sentences.
- Explain yesterday’s AI concept in 5 sentences.
- Check the source only after attempting recall.

### Wednesday — Feynman
Explain one concept as if teaching a colleague who knows nothing about it:
1. Explain in English for 2 minutes.
2. Explain in Chinese if stuck.
3. Explain again in simpler English.

If it cannot be explained simply, it is not understood well enough.

### Thursday — Apply
Use real work as learning material.

English examples:
- Draft supplier emails first.
- Ask AI to identify mistakes before giving a polished version.
- Save 2–3 useful expressions from each correction.

AI examples:
- HS classification research.
- Logistics quotation comparison.
- RFQ analysis.
- Invoice and packing-list checks.
- Supplier sourcing.
- Cost analysis.

### Friday — Test
Do not review first.
- Recall the week’s English expressions.
- Create sentences with 10 random items.
- Explain the week’s AI concept for 5 minutes in English.
- Write a 150-word English summary.
- Answer 10 AI questions.

Track:
- Remembered
- Forgot
- Don’t understand

### Saturday — Project
Build one small visible output every week.

Examples:
- Week 1: explain Yinghao’s work in English.
- Week 4: build a supplier-email assistant workflow.
- Week 8: use Python + AI to analyse a purchasing CSV.
- Week 12: prototype a supply-chain AI assistant.

### Sunday — Review
1. Spaced repetition.
2. Delete low-value notes.
3. Choose next week’s English topic and AI concept.

Suggested review spacing:

Day 1 → Day 3 → Day 7 → Day 14 → Day 30 → Day 60

Always recall first; look at the answer second.

## 90-Day Roadmap

### Days 1–30 — Foundation

#### English
Focus on:
- High-frequency verbs.
- Useful collocations.
- Business English.
- Common grammar errors.
- Pronunciation.
- Listening.
- Simple spoken explanations.

Priority expressions include:
advise, confirm, arrange, proceed, require, provide, follow up, resolve, available, regarding, subject to, according to, in terms of.

Pay special attention to:
- Tense.
- Articles.
- Prepositions.
- Singular/plural forms.
- Word choice.
- Sentence structure.

Target: 5–8 useful expressions per day, with genuine active recall.

#### AI
- Week 1: AI vs ML vs LLM.
- Week 2: token, context window, hallucination.
- Week 3: Transformer, training, inference.
- Week 4: prompting, reasoning and tools.

Goal: explain concepts clearly rather than memorise definitions.

### Days 31–60 — Capability Building

English loop:

> Input → Recall → Speak → Correct → Repeat

Use 5–10 minute English content, then close it and verbally explain what was understood.

AI topics:
- Embeddings.
- Vector databases.
- RAG.
- AI agents.
- APIs.
- JSON.
- Python basics.
- Automation.

Use the learning loop:

> Concept → Explain → Build

### Days 61–90 — Practical Use

Reduce passive AI study and increase real problem-solving.

Suggested capstone: **Supply Chain AI Assistant**.

Inputs may include:
- Supplier quotation.
- PO.
- Commercial invoice.
- Packing list.

Possible functions:
- Extract part numbers.
- Compare prices.
- Detect quantity mismatches.
- Check invoices.
- Summarise quotations.
- Generate supplier questions.
- Identify anomalies.
- Export structured results to Excel or reports.

Learn Python, APIs, JSON, OCR, structured output, validation and automation only when the project creates a need for them.

## Daily Minimum

### 30-minute version
- 10 min English listening/reading.
- 5 min active recall.
- 10 min AI concept.
- 5 min Feynman explanation in English.

### 60-minute version
- 20 min English.
- 20 min AI.
- 10 min active recall.
- 10 min Feynman/speaking.

Never skip a day just because a full hour is unavailable.

## Obsidian Structure

```text
English
├── Vocabulary
├── Mistakes
└── Speaking

AI
├── Concepts
├── Questions
└── Projects
```

The highest-value English note is **English Mistakes**.

Example:

```text
❌ Could you advise weather you can help?
✅ Could you advise whether you can help?

❌ I have sent yesterday.
✅ I sent it yesterday.

❌ When you reckon it will ready?
✅ When do you reckon it will be ready?
```

Re-test old mistakes every Friday.

## How to Use ChatGPT as a Tutor

Instead of asking:

> Explain Transformer to me.

Use:

> Ask me five questions to test my understanding of Transformer. Ask them one at a time. Do not give me the answer immediately. Identify my knowledge gaps from my responses and then teach only what I am missing.

For English, instead of immediately asking for a rewrite:

> First identify my mistakes and let me correct them myself. Do not give me the final answer yet. If my second attempt is still wrong, explain why and then show the correct version.

This turns AI from an answer machine into a tutor.

## Learning Formula

> Learn 20% → Recall 30% → Explain 20% → Apply 30%

The 90-day success criterion is not course completion or vocabulary count. It is the ability to learn something in English, understand it, explain it in your own words, and use AI to turn that knowledge into something useful.
